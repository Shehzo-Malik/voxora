Voxora - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026. Voxora is an AI assistant app developed by Muhammad Shehzal Abbas ("we", "us").

What we collect

  • Your messages and attachments. When you chat, your text (and any image or file you attach) is sent to an AI service to produce a reply: Google Gemini (through our secure server) and, as a backup, a free third-party AI service (Pollinations).
  • Place searches. If you ask about a place or distance, the place name is sent to OpenStreetMap (Nominatim / OSRM) to look up map data.
  • Account details. If you sign in with Google or with a name and email, we store your name, email and profile picture only in your own browser or device so the app can show your account.
  • Chats and memory. Your chat history and "memory" notes are saved locally on your own device. We do not keep them on our servers.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data and we do not show ads. We do not ask for your phone contacts, location or microphone.

Your choices

You can delete a chat, clear your memory, or sign out at any time inside the app. Clearing your browser or app data removes everything stored on your device. Please do not share passwords, card numbers or other sensitive information in chats.

AI content

Answers are generated by AI and may contain mistakes. Do not rely on them for medical, legal or financial decisions. To report an offensive or harmful answer, email us at the address below.

Children

Voxora is not directed at children under 13.
